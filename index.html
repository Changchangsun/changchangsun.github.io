About Me

I am a PhD Candidate (expected graduation: May 2027) in the Computer Vision and Multimedia Laboratory (CVMLab) at the University of Illinois Chicago (UIC), supervised by Prof. Yan Yan. Before joining UIC, I studied at the Illinois Institute of Technology (IIT) (Aug. 2021-Dec. 2024, transferred to UIC with my advisor), supervised by Prof. Yan Yan, and was a visiting student (Aug. 2023-May 2025) at Michigan State University under the supervision of Prof. Sijia Liu. Before that, I received my Master's and Bachelor's degrees in Computer Science and Technology from Shandong University in 2021 and 2018, supervised by Prof. Xuemeng Song and co-supervised by Prof. Liqiang Nie. I have also interned as a research scientist at TikTok (Summer 2026) and Pony.ai (Summer 2025).

My research interests include:

  • Generative AI: dance-to-music generation with latent diffusion models;
  • Trustworthy ML: machine unlearning;
  • Computer Vision: human-object interaction detection;
  • Multimedia Retrieval: cross-modal hashing.

I am currently on the job market for academic and industrial positions. Please see my CV and Google Scholar. Feel free to reach out if you think I could be a good fit.
